Our engagement models

Choose the right way to work with us.

01

Project Delivery

Defined scope. Clear deliverables. A planned finish.

For projects with specific requirements, agreed outputs, and a defined timeline.

We manage the agreed delivery scope from planning and execution through quality checks and handover, giving your team a clear view of what needs to be delivered and when.

Discuss a Project →

Best suited for

  • Platform implementations and configurations
  • Campaign or digital production projects
  • Automation and integration requirements
  • Data processing and engineering tasks
  • Technical improvements and defined QA work

How it works

  1. Define the requirements and deliverables
  2. Agree on scope, timeline, milestones, and pricing
  3. Execute and validate the agreed work
  4. Handover the completed outputs and documentation

Commercial approach

Typically priced against an agreed scope, milestones, or project fee. Payment schedules and any deposit requirements are confirmed in the proposal or statement of work.

Once the agreed project is completed and handed over, there is no automatic ongoing service commitment.

Additional work can be scoped separately.

02

Ongoing Service

Consistent support. Recurring delivery.

For teams that need recurring digital work, operational support, or continued assistance beyond a single project.

We establish an ongoing scope and delivery rhythm that supports your team’s priorities while providing a clear basis for planning and managing recurring work.

Discuss Ongoing Support →

Best suited for

  • Marketing technology operations
  • Campaign production and deployment support
  • Recurring QA and validation
  • Automation and integration maintenance
  • Data workflow monitoring and support
  • Ongoing technical troubleshooting

How it works

  1. Identify recurring requirements and priorities
  2. Define the service scope and expected outputs
  3. Agree on the service cadence and reporting approach
  4. Review priorities and adjust the scope through an agreed process

Commercial approach

Typically structured around a recurring monthly fee based on the agreed scope, service level, and expected workload.

The service terms will specify the billing cycle, included work, exclusions, and cancellation notice. Where offered on a month-to-month basis, the engagement can end with the agreed notice rather than requiring an indefinite commitment.

Additional work outside the agreed scope may require separate approval and pricing.

03

Delivery Pod

Coordinated delivery capacity for ongoing needs.

For teams that require sustained delivery capacity across a defined set of workstreams, projects, or operational priorities.

GI coordinates the delivery approach and appropriate resources around an agreed scope and capacity arrangement. The focus is on getting the work done—not simply supplying people.

Discuss a Delivery Pod →

Best suited for

  • Multiple related delivery workstreams
  • Sustained campaign or platform operations
  • Recurring technical implementation work
  • Ongoing automation and data initiatives
  • Teams with a continuing pipeline of defined requirements

How it works

  1. Understand your delivery pipeline and capacity needs
  2. Define the scope, priorities, and expected capacity
  3. Establish responsibilities, workflows, and communication
  4. Coordinate delivery, quality checks, and progress reviews

Commercial approach

Typically structured around a recurring fee for an agreed level of delivery capacity and scope.

The proposal will define the capacity arrangement, term, included work, prioritization, availability, and treatment of unused capacity. Any minimum commitment or notice period will be agreed before work begins, particularly where GI needs to reserve resources or make non-cancellable commitments.

A Delivery Pod is a delivery arrangement, not an automatic transfer of personnel into your organization.

04

White-Label Delivery

Your client relationship. Our delivery support.

For agencies and partners that need a dependable delivery partner working behind the scenes under their brand or agreed client-facing arrangement.

GI supports the delivery of specialized digital work while you maintain the client relationship. Responsibilities, visibility, communication, and handover arrangements are defined for each engagement.

Discuss a Partnership →

Best suited for

  • Agencies that need additional delivery capability
  • Partners with specialized work outside their core team
  • Agencies managing multiple client projects
  • Teams seeking a reliable behind-the-scenes delivery partner
  • Recurring or project-based client work requiring additional execution support

How it works

  1. Align on your end-client requirements and expectations
  2. Agree on roles, scope, communication, and brand visibility
  3. Define the delivery workflow and quality requirements
  4. Execute, validate, and hand over work according to the agreed arrangement

Commercial approach

White-label delivery can be structured as a project, ongoing service, or Delivery Pod, depending on the requirements.

The agreement defines pricing, payment terms, client communication, confidentiality, intellectual property, responsibilities, and any restrictions on disclosure or direct client contact.

We align delivery commitments with the agreed scope so that responsibilities are clear for both GI and the partner.

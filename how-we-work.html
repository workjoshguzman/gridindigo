How we work

From requirement to delivery.

A clearer way to get work done

Structured for delivery. Flexible by design.

Good delivery takes more than technical expertise. It requires a clear understanding of the requirement, realistic planning, the right capabilities, and accountability from start to finish.

Our approach gives every engagement a defined direction while allowing the delivery model to adapt to your needs, priorities, and existing environment.

Three principles guide our work:

Clarity before execution

We align on the requirements, scope, expected outputs, responsibilities, and success criteria before work begins.

The right capability for the requirement

We determine the expertise, tools, and delivery approach needed for the agreed scope rather than forcing every project into the same process.

Accountability throughout delivery

We maintain clear communication, validate the work against agreed requirements, and document the outputs and next steps.

From requirement to delivery

One process. Clear responsibilities. Defined outcomes.

Every engagement follows a structured approach, adapted to the complexity and requirements of the work.

1

Understand the Requirement

We start by understanding the problem, the desired outcome, and the environment in which the work needs to happen.

We clarify your objectives, existing systems, constraints, dependencies, and expectations before recommending an approach.

What happens

  • Discuss your goals and requirements
  • Review relevant systems, workflows, and documentation
  • Identify constraints, dependencies, and open questions
  • Establish the desired outcomes and success criteria

Outcome

A shared understanding of the requirement and what success looks like.

2

Define the Scope

Once the requirement is clear, we define what needs to be delivered, how it will be approached, and what is required to complete the work.

We align on deliverables, timelines, responsibilities, assumptions, exclusions, and commercial terms before execution begins.

What happens

  • Define deliverables and acceptance criteria
  • Establish milestones and timelines
  • Identify required capabilities, access, and dependencies
  • Confirm pricing, responsibilities, and scope boundaries

Outcome

An agreed scope with clear expectations before work begins.

3

Assemble the Delivery Approach

We determine the appropriate delivery setup based on the requirement, complexity, timeline, and agreed engagement model.

This may involve existing internal capabilities, specialized delivery resources, relevant technology, reusable processes, and automation where appropriate.

What happens

  • Determine the skills and resources required
  • Select suitable tools and technologies
  • Establish the workflow and communication approach
  • Plan execution, dependencies, and quality checks

Outcome

A delivery plan aligned with the scope and required capabilities.

4

Execute and Manage Delivery

With the scope and approach established, we move into execution.

We coordinate the work, track progress, manage dependencies, communicate relevant updates, and address issues that could affect the agreed deliverables.

What happens

  • Execute the agreed work
  • Coordinate tasks and dependencies
  • Communicate progress and emerging risks
  • Manage changes through an agreed process

Outcome

Work delivered against the agreed scope, timeline, and requirements.

5

QA, Handoff & Improve

Before delivery is considered complete, we check the outputs against the agreed requirements and acceptance criteria, then close the loop with a clear handover.

Validation depends on the nature of the work, from functional checks and data validation to campaign testing and implementation reviews. Depending on the engagement, handover may mean returning the work to your team, supporting deployment, or continuing into an ongoing service arrangement.

What happens

  • Verify outputs against agreed requirements
  • Perform relevant functional and quality checks, and coordinate corrections
  • Deliver the agreed outputs with documentation and handover notes
  • Capture lessons learned and agree next steps or ongoing support

Outcome

Validated outputs, a clear handover, and a defined path forward.

AI, automation & human accountability

Smarter with technology. Accountable by people.

We use technology to support efficient delivery, while human judgment, technical expertise, and quality assurance help ensure the work meets its requirements.

AI and automation can assist with analysis, repetitive tasks, documentation, and repeatable workflows. People remain responsible for decisions, validation, risk management, and the quality of the agreed deliverables.

Technology accelerates

We use appropriate AI-assisted workflows and automation to support:

  • Requirement analysis and information organization
  • Repetitive and structured tasks
  • Workflow design and process improvement
  • Technical research and documentation
  • Test planning and quality-check preparation

People stay accountable

Human expertise and oversight guide:

  • Scope, solution, and delivery decisions
  • Technical review and validation
  • Quality checks and risk assessment
  • Client communication and expectation management
  • Acceptance, handover, and delivery accountability

The principle behind our approach

Technology supports the work. People remain accountable for the outcome.

We choose tools based on the requirements, existing environment, security considerations, and agreed scope—not simply because a technology is available.

Built into the delivery process

Quality is part of delivery, not an afterthought.

Quality assurance begins with clear requirements and continues throughout execution. We define what needs to be checked, validate the outputs, and address identified issues according to the agreed scope and acceptance criteria.

The checks applied to each engagement depend on the work being delivered, its technical requirements, and its associated risks.

Our goal is to make delivery more predictable, make issues easier to identify, and give your team clarity about what has been completed and what remains outstanding.

Our approach may include:

Requirement and scope validation
Functional and implementation testing
Data quality and reconciliation checks
Campaign and email validation
Defect documentation and retesting
Release readiness and handover checks
Clear ownership. Fewer surprises.

You should always know where the work stands.

Effective delivery depends on more than completing tasks. It also requires clear communication, visible responsibilities, and a practical way to manage changes.

We establish the communication and reporting approach at the beginning of each engagement, based on its size, duration, and complexity.

Clear points of contact

Know who coordinates the engagement, where questions should go, and who is responsible for key decisions.

Progress and risk visibility

Receive relevant updates on progress, dependencies, blockers, and issues that may affect delivery.

Controlled scope changes

New requirements and changes are assessed for their impact on deliverables, timelines, and cost before they are incorporated.

Defined completion criteria

We align on what completion means, how outputs will be validated, and what will be handed over.

The result is a more structured working relationship, with fewer assumptions and clearer expectations on both sides.

The delivery approach should fit the work

Different requirements call for different ways of working.

Some requirements are clearly defined and can be delivered as a one-time project. Others need recurring support or dedicated delivery capacity over time.

We work with you to identify an engagement model that fits the requirement, expected duration, delivery needs, and level of commitment.

Project Delivery

For defined requirements with agreed deliverables, scope, and timelines.

Ongoing Service

For recurring work that benefits from continued support and operational consistency.

Delivery Pod

For engagements that require coordinated delivery capacity across an agreed period.

White-Label Delivery

For agencies and partners that need delivery support behind their own client relationships and brand.

The delivery process is adapted to the engagement. Scope, capacity, timelines, responsibilities, and commercial terms are agreed before work begins.

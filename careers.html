Roles we’re building for.

Joining the talent pool is not a job offer or a guarantee of work. Roles open as client projects are confirmed, and every engagement is agreed in writing first.

Remote · Full-time when available · Fresh grads and career shifters welcome

Junior Digital Specialist

Learn real client delivery from day one: building emails and pages, updating websites, and supporting marketing platform work, with every deliverable checked before it reaches the client.

What you’ll work on

  • Building and updating HTML emails and landing pages
  • Website and CMS content updates, and localizing content for new markets
  • Supporting marketing platform work, such as Salesforce Marketing Cloud
  • Reviewing each other’s work before it reaches the client

What we look for

  • Strong attention to detail
  • Clear written English and comfort talking with clients
  • Basic HTML/CSS, design or website experience is a plus, not a must
  • You meet deadlines and speak up early when something is at risk

Remote · On-call and project-based · Paid hourly by invoice

Senior Consultant – Marketing Technology

Lead complex marketing platform work for our clients and guide the specialists who build alongside you. No fixed hours and no minimum commitment.

What you’ll work on

  • Complex builds: journeys, automations, workflows, AMPscript/SSJS and SQL
  • Reviewing and guiding the work of our specialists
  • Technical input when we scope and estimate client work

What we look for

  • 3+ years of hands-on Salesforce Marketing Cloud or Adobe Campaign Classic work
  • Experience with enterprise clients and production deadlines
  • Adobe Journey Optimizer, CDP or API integration experience is a plus

Grid Indigo never asks applicants for any fee or payment, and only contacts applicants from our official channels. If someone asks you to pay to join, it isn’t us.
